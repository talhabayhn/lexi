# ADR 0007: Freemium: rewarded ads + Lexi Pro via RevenueCat

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14

## Context
Learning apps monetize best with subscriptions; free players should never be blocked by ads mid-game.

## Decision
AdMob rewarded ads for jokers (+ at most one interstitial per 3 finished games, never in-game); Lexi Pro monthly/yearly/lifetime through RevenueCat KMP.

## Consequences
RevenueCat handles receipts and cross-platform entitlements; small revenue share above free tier.
