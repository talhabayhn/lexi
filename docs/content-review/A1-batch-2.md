## Judgement calls

- Q/X/Z not extended: Q, X, Z already have 3-4 A1 cards each; only ZEBRA added (Z, STARTS, difficulty 2).
- 60 old examples were rewritten (batch 1 and sample) because they contained new batch-2 answers as whole words (e.g. "The next bus is at ten", "The sun is yellow"). Clues/answers unchanged.
- Category: no clothes category exists, so JACKET/JEANS/SHIRT/HAT/DRESS/SHOE are in `daily-life`. BIG/TALL/KIND/NICE-type adjectives sit in `family-people`/`daily-life`.
- ENGLISH: answer is written ENGLISH (id a1-english); example capitalises the word, which the compiler accepts case-insensitively.
- INTO / UNDER / UP / ONLY / USUALLY: function words with approximate Turkish clues ("bir şeyin içine doğru", "altında"); pos set to phrase/adv as in the example. Could be dropped if too abstract for a clue game.
- INTERNET, VIDEO, RADIO, HOTEL, DOCTOR, MUSIC, ZEBRA, OFFICE: near-cognates; clues are descriptions (not the Turkish word) to avoid giveaways.
- EAT ("yemek yemek") vs COOK ("pişirmek") vs FOOD ("yiyecek"): kept distinct; EAT/COOK are first "yemek"-adjacent so please confirm.
- DAD ("baba (konuşma dilinde)") vs existing FATHER ("baba"); KID ("çocuk (günlük dil)") vs BOY/GIRL; WIFE "karısı, evli kadın" - near-synonym clues to review.
- OIL "sıvı yağ", KEEP "tutmak, saklamak", LOVE "sevmek, aşık olmak" are broad; confirm.
- CEFR: INTERNET, USUALLY, KEEP, INTO, OIL, ZEBRA may be A2 in some lists; included for letter balance (I, U, K, O, Z).
- Compiler: 0 errors; the only warning (A2 has 2 sample cards, 26 letters below 3) was already present and unrelated.
