## Review changes (2026-11-12, code review)

- Removed: TRAINERS (near-synonym of a2-sneakers), PHOTOGRAPH (A1 word, same clue as a1-photo), BASEBALL (level not
  confirmed as A2).
- Kept in daily-life (not moved): weight, design, almost (examples are not on theme) and the feelings glad, afraid,
  bored, excited, relaxed, unhappy, alone (A1 keeps feelings in daily-life; only personality words go to
  family-people).
- LOSE goes to travel (its example is about losing a ticket on a trip), not sports.
- Clues: POP "gençlerin sevdiği, radyoda çok çalan müzik türü", CURLY "kıvır kıvır olan saç için", PAST "şu andan
  önceki zaman, eski günler".
