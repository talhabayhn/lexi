# Release notes 1.1.5

Consolidated store release notes covering everything since **1.0.13** (assumed to be the last version published to the stores: includes 1.0.14, 1.0.15, 1.1.0–1.1.4 and 1.1.5).
Limits: App Store What's New ≤ 4000, Promotional Text ≤ 170, Play release notes ≤ 500 characters per language.

## What changed since 1.0.13
- Simpler Home: total score, the OYNA / PLAY card (three inline mode boxes removed), study section and bottom bar; daily gift notice can also be swiped up to dismiss; "Invite a friend" uses the system share sheet.
- New "Çarkını seç" / "Pick your wheel" flow: tap a category card to flip into the mode and time dialog (Classic and Hardcore 3, 5 or 7 minutes, last choice remembered), tap OYNA / PLAY, and watch the first letter morph into the wheel.
- More categories with balanced word lists: 8 new categories with new art (and art for every category), 42 new words, thin categories merged or removed, every wheel filled to 26 letters, and categories without words at the chosen level hidden.
- Smoother first launch on iPhone: native Core Animation launch splash committed before warm-up completes, plus card flip playing in Low Power Mode.
- Smoother transitions and small fixes: fail-safe timeouts on animations/transitions, shadows appearing together with elements, picker "Başa dön" / "Back to top" scrolling to the real top, equal card heights per row with the flipping row kept on top, Reduce Motion, large text support, and optional portrait feature on Android.
