# Lexi — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 3 November 2026 · **Applies to:** the Lexi app for Android and iOS, version 1.0.5 and later (app ID `dev.talhabayhan.lexi`)

## 1. Who we are
The data controller is **Hüseyin Talha Bayhan** ("we"). Contact: **talhabayhan.aps@gmail.com** · **https://talhabayhn.github.io/lexi**.

## 2. Summary
- Lexi works offline. There is **no account** and no sign-up.
- Your game progress, settings and word notebook are stored **only on your device**. We do not receive them.
- The only third-party service in the app is **Google Firebase**: **Crashlytics** (crash reports, always on) and **Analytics** (usage statistics, **off until you agree**).
- Nothing is sold. Nothing is used for advertising or for tracking you across other apps or websites. The app contains **no ads** and uses **no advertising ID** and **no location**.
- The app is **not directed to children**.

## 3. Data stored only on your device
Progress, scores, streaks, joker inventory, word notebook, statistics and settings (language, sound, haptics, reminder, daily goal). We do not access this data. Deleting the app (or clearing app data) removes it. On Android, your device's own backup (for example Google backup) may copy app data according to your backup settings.

## 4. Data sent to Firebase
| Service | Data | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase Analytics | A random app-instance ID (on iOS also the vendor identifier), device model, OS and app version, language, country-level location, and in-app events: app opened, onboarding finished, level test result, game started and finished (mode, level, correct answers, duration), joker used (type), result shared, reminder turned on. Sessions and first-open are counted by Firebase itself. No names, no free text, no advertising ID. | Understand how the app is used and improve it | **Only after you agree**: tick "Share anonymous usage statistics" on the last onboarding step, or turn on **Settings → Usage statistics**. Until then nothing is sent. Turn the switch off at any time and collection stops. |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Crash and error traces, a short fixed description of what failed (never your input), device model, OS version, app version, app state at the crash, and a random installation ID | Find and fix crashes, keep the app stable | In the store version of the app, always. It is not controlled by the usage-statistics switch. |

Firebase is operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited under its own terms. When the app contacts Google, Google's servers receive your IP address as part of the connection.

## 5. What we do not do
- No advertising ID is read (on Android the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app; on iOS the advertising identifier is not used and the app does not show the tracking prompt).
- Ad personalisation and ad storage are switched off in the Firebase settings of the app. Analytics data is not shared with advertising services and is not used to build advertising profiles.
- The app does not request location permission, does not read contacts, photos or files, and does not record audio.
- Nothing is sold or rented to anyone.

## 6. Notifications
If you enable the daily reminder, notifications are scheduled **locally on your device**. We do not use push servers. Permission can be revoked in system settings.

## 7. Retention
- On-device data: until you delete the app or its data.
- Firebase Analytics event data: kept for 2 months in our Firebase settings, then deleted by Google.
- Crashlytics crash data: kept by Google for about 90 days.

## 8. International transfers
Google may process data outside your country (for example in the USA). Google relies on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and/or adequacy decisions.

## 9. Your rights and how to ask for deletion
Under **GDPR** (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdraw consent at any time, complaint to your supervisory authority) and **KVKK Art. 11** (learn whether data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and the recipients, request correction or deletion, object to results, claim compensation) you may write to **talhabayhan.aps@gmail.com**; we reply within 30 days.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn off Settings → Usage statistics. This stops further analytics collection.
- **Deletion:** email us. We hold no name, email or account for you, and the identifiers above are random and not shown in the app, so we cannot look you up by yourself. Uninstalling the app ends collection, and Firebase deletes the data after the retention periods in §7.
- You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local supervisory authority.

## 10. Children
Lexi is **not directed to children** and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child gave us data, contact us and we will help delete it.

## 11. Security
Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). On-device data sits in the app's private storage. No method is perfectly secure.

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy. The date above changes and, for material changes (for example new services such as ads or purchases), we will notify you in the app before they take effect.
