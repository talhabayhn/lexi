## Google Play

**Title (≤30)**
```
Lexi: English Alphabet Quiz
```

**Short description (≤80)**
```
Learn English words with a fun A–Z wheel. Turkish clues, A1 to native level.
```

**Full description (≤4000)**
```
Lexi turns English vocabulary into a game you actually want to play.

HOW IT WORKS
Every letter of the alphabet is a challenge. You read a clue in Turkish, think of the English word that starts with (or contains) the current letter, and type it with the letter keys. Not sure? Pass and come back to it on the next lap. Clear the whole A–Z wheel before time runs out!

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL
• A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and Native (idioms, phrasal verbs)
• Word categories inside each level, plus a mixed wheel
• A short placement test helps you find your starting level

GAME MODES
• Classic – race the clock
• Practice – no timer, no pressure
• Hardcore – one mistake ends the game
• Daily Wheel – a new wheel every day, the same for everyone
• Review Wheel – replay the words you need to practise

LEARN, NOT JUST PLAY
• Review list at the end of every game
• Personal word notebook with spaced repetition
• LGS exam word pack (8th grade English), unit by unit
• Listen to pronunciation with your device's text-to-speech voice
• Stats: learned words, accuracy, streaks, weekly progress, hardest letters
• Daily goal, optional daily and review reminders, and a share card for your results

JOKERS
Stuck? Use a joker: reveal a letter, remove distractor keys, see an example sentence or listen to the word. Earn jokers daily and with streaks.

WORKS OFFLINE
All word content is stored on your device. You do not need an account or an internet connection to play.

FREE, NO ADS
Lexi is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

PRIVACY
Your progress stays on your device. No account needed. Anonymous usage statistics are sent only if you agree (you can change this in Settings); crash reports help us fix bugs. Details in our privacy policy.

The interface is available in Turkish and English.

Questions or feedback: talhabayhan.aps@gmail.com
https://talhabayhn.github.io/lexi
```

**What's new (v1.0)**
```
Welcome to Lexi! Play the A–Z word wheel in six levels, practise offline, take on the daily wheel and share your results.
```
