## 3. Pro structure recommendation

Fits `docs/PRODUCT.md`: Pro = no ads, exam packs, +3 jokers refill, unlimited review wheels, (v1.1) create rooms. Three SKUs, one entitlement `pro`.

- **Yearly = hero** (default selected, "best value"). Subscription fatigue and low TR purchasing power argue for a clear annual saving of ≥ 50% vs 12× monthly.
- **Monthly = anchor/escape hatch**: exists mainly to make yearly look good, and for exam crammers (YDS/YÖKDİL has two sittings a year, so seasonal monthly demand is likely; assumption, not measured).
- **Lifetime = non-consumable**: strong fit for TR (subscription-averse, FX-hedging users) and for a finite content set. Risk: ongoing content/hosting costs → keep lifetime ≥ ~2.2–2.5× yearly and promise "all current and future exam packs" only if you intend to ship them (decision for owner; define "lifetime" in terms: life of the app).

### Three TRY price options (proposals, not market data)

Reasoning anchors: WordUp ~₺70/₺400 (S, unverified), Duolingo yearly ~₺840 (S, conflicting) as ceiling, exam apps at USD $1–4/mo (low floor), plus .99 price endings.

| Option | Monthly | Yearly | Lifetime | Yearly vs 12×monthly | Reasoning | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Match (recommended start)** | ₺79.99 | ₺449.99 | ₺999.99 | −53% | Slightly above the closest local vocab competitor, justified by exam packs + ad-free; stays at about half of Duolingo's yearly | If the WordUp anchor is stale/lower, A is expensive |
| **B. Penetration** | ₺59.99 | ₺349.99 | ₺799.99 | −51% | Undercuts the nearest rival; maximises installs→paid for a new, unknown brand | Hard to raise later (consent flows); leaves margin on the table |
| **C. Exam-premium** | ₺99.99 | ₺599.99 | ₺1,299.99 | −50% | Positions around the exam value (prep courses cost ₺1,500–7,500) | Free exam lists and cheap USD-priced rivals make this a hard sell for a v1.0 brand |

Recommendation: **A at launch, with TR localisations of the paywall copy anchored on price-per-month ("₺37,50/ay" yearly)**, review at day 60. Because Apple subscription prices don't auto-adjust (§2), a set price decays in real terms with the lira; schedule a review and an *increase for new subscribers while preserving existing ones* when needed.

### Trial / intro offer
- Yearly: **7-day free trial** (both stores support per-territory trials; eligibility must be checked before showing trial copy, per RevenueCat community guidance [S](https://community.revenuecat.com/general-questions-7/introductory-offer-for-a-subscription-is-not-shown-for-some-countries-1694)). Trial on yearly only, none on monthly or lifetime.
- Optional exam-season promo: a **pay-up-front intro** (e.g. first-year intro price) via promotional offers, only after baseline conversion is known.
- Don't gate the first session; show the paywall after the first completed wheel or at a locked exam pack (consistent with the "never in the first session" ad rule in PRODUCT.md).
- Store listing text already says "Free trial, if offered, is forfeited…" (listing-en.md) so a trial is compatible with the drafts.

### Regional notes (diaspora DE/NL/US)
- Don't hand-set EUR/USD at launch: use each store's equalised conversion from your base storefront, then override only where it looks off. Suggested *starting guesses* to compare against the equalised output (not market data): monthly €2.99–3.99 / $3.99; yearly €19.99–24.99 / $24.99; lifetime €39.99–49.99 / $49.99. Check these against what the stores generate before accepting any.
- Diaspora users are the higher-ARPU segment; TR price + equalised EU price means price discrimination by storefront, which is standard and allowed. Decide whether to base-price in TRY (Apple warns auto-adjusts won't apply to the base storefront for IAP) or in EUR/USD with TR overridden — recommend **USD/EUR base, TR manually managed**, because lifetime IAP in TR then won't silently drift.
- Store region ≠ language; a user with a TR clue UI in Berlin is billed in EUR. Never infer price from UI language.

### Revisiting TRY pricing
1. Calendar review monthly for first 3 months, then quarterly.
2. Trigger a review if TRY moves >10% vs USD since the last set (assumption: threshold of your choosing).
3. Keep a small price-change log in this folder; changes for subscriptions: schedule increase for new subs and preserve existing, mind the consent rules (§2).
4. Re-check competitor TRY prices at each review (the numbers in §1 are stale on arrival).

### RevenueCat setup implications
- Products created in both consoles: `lexi_pro_monthly`, `lexi_pro_yearly` (one subscription group/subscription each) and `lexi_pro_lifetime` (non-consumable on Apple, one-time product on Play). One entitlement `pro`; one offering `default` with packages monthly/annual/lifetime (RevenueCat docs could not be fetched here; this is the standard pattern — confirm against current docs).
- **Never hardcode prices** (PRODUCT.md rule): paywall shows `storeProduct.localizedPriceString`, and compute "per month" with the store's currency formatting.
- Trial display: check intro-offer eligibility before showing trial wording.
- Add a second offering (`exam_season`) later for experiments without an app release; RevenueCat experiments need enough traffic to be meaningful (low at launch → don't A/B early).
- Free-trial/lifetime restore: implement "Restore purchases" (App Review requirement) and handle Play grace period.
- TR tax/VAT is the stores' job; revenue reports in RevenueCat are net of store cut but show TRY→USD conversion at report time, so FX will show up in revenue charts.

## 4. Assumptions and uncertainty
- Competitor TRY prices are secondary/undated; two of them (Duolingo, WordUp) conflict or are unreadable.
- No evidence collected on conversion rates; the 2% Pro target in PRODUCT.md is untested.
- The TRY options are judgment-based proposals, not derived from willingness-to-pay data.
- All dates: sources not individually dated unless stated; anything pre-Oct-2025 (Android blog 2021, Webrazzi 2015) is flagged as >12 months old.
